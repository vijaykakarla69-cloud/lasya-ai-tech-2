The diagnosis

Why you're not ranking for city-qualified searches

Three findings account for nearly all of the gap between where you are and "best mobile app / website development company in HITEC City, Madhapur, Jubilee Hills, Kondapur." First, the structured data shipped on every page has no local-business signal at all — the Organization schema carries a name, URL, logo and social links, but no address, phone, geo-coordinates or service area. Second, a full-repo search turns up zero pages, headings or paragraphs anywhere on the site that name HITEC City, Jubilee Hills or Kondapur — the only place "Hyderabad" appears in visible copy is the office-address block on /contact. Third, nothing on the site links to a Google Business Profile, which is the single strongest input into exactly the "best X company near me" ranking you're asking for.

None of this requires a rebuild. The metadata system already in place (SeoService.ts, page-seo.ts, seo-keywords.ts) is well-engineered — it just has no location layer yet. Phase 1 below adds one.

One expectation to set correctly

The bare head term "best mobile app development company" — no location — is contested globally by Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush and similar review aggregators, which dominate that SERP because they aggregate ratings across thousands of agencies. No single agency's own domain typically outranks them for the undecorated head term, and that's true for every competitor you have too.

The target that's actually winnable in 60–90 days of focused work is the layer directly below it: the Google Maps 3-pack for "app development company near me" searched from those neighborhoods, and long-tail organic terms like "mobile app development company Madhapur" or "website development company near HITEC City." That's exactly what Phases 1–2 target. Ranking well on Clutch/GoodFirms (Phase 3) is still worth doing on top of that — those profiles increasingly get cited directly inside Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity answers, which is a second route to head-term visibility that doesn't require outranking them.

What's already working

Worth knowing before you change anything — this is genuinely more sophisticated than the "20 SEO plugins" framing suggests. This is a Next.js site with a hand-built SEO system, not WordPress, and no plugin marketplace applies here; the equivalent work already exists in code.

  • Every route uses Next.js generateMetadata through one shared service, so titles/descriptions/Open Graph/Twitter cards/canonicals are consistent and clamped to SERP-safe lengths instead of being hand-typed per page. src/services/SeoService.ts:44–80
  • next-sitemap tiers priority and crawl frequency by route importance, and keeps campaign/demo pages out of the sitemap via noindex rather than blocking them in robots.txt — which would stop crawlers from ever reading that noindex tag. next-sitemap.config.js
  • robots.txt explicitly allows GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and other AI crawlers, and a hand-maintained llms.txt already indexes every service, technology and industry page — a real head start on AI-search visibility most competitors won't have. public/robots.txt, public/llms.txt
  • Structured data already covers Organization, WebPage, Service, FAQPage, Article (case studies), JobPosting (careers) and BreadcrumbList. src/services/SeoService.ts:259–439
  • A real office address, phone number, WhatsApp link and embedded Google Map already render on /contact. The raw NAP data exists — it just isn't connected to structured data or repeated anywhere else on the site. src/components/common/ContactSection.tsx:180, 195, 404–416

Phase 2

On-page & content

Weeks 3–6

Goal: put HITEC City, Madhapur, Jubilee Hills and Kondapur into real, visible content — titles, headings, one authoritative page — without creating doorway pages that would actively hurt more than help.

Don't do this instead Building four near-identical pages — /locations/hitec-city, /locations/madhapur, /locations/jubilee-hills, /locations/kondapur — with the city name swapped and everything else the same is a doorway-page pattern, which Google's spam policy targets directly. It would rank worse than having zero location pages, not better. One strong, detailed page beats four thin clones.

Phase 3

Authority, reviews & polish

Weeks 7–12

Goal: earn the signals only time and third parties can give — reviews, citations, backlinks — and clear the Core Web Vitals debt that compounds local rank over the following quarter.

DirectoryWhy it's worth claiming
Google Business ProfilePrimary local-pack input — see Phase 1.
Clutch / GoodFirms / DesignRushDominates "best X development company" SERPs and is increasingly cited inside AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity answers — a second route to head-term visibility.
Justdial / Sulekha / IndiaMARTIndia-specific B2B directories that carry real local-citation weight for Hyderabad searches.
LinkedIn Company PageNAP-consistent citation plus a channel your sameAs schema already references.

Google Business Profile: the compliant way to cover four neighborhoods

Your office is genuinely in Madhapur, on HITEC City Main Road — so those two are straightforward: they're your real address. Jubilee Hills and Kondapur are nearby but are not where your office is, and that distinction matters for how you configure this.

Google suspends listings that claim a second physical address you don't actually staff. The compliant path — and the one that gets you found in Jubilee Hills and Kondapur searches without risking your listing — is enabling GBP's service-area setting on top of your existing storefront address, then adding those neighborhoods as service areas. That's a supported, standard configuration for exactly this situation: one real office, several nearby areas you serve.
