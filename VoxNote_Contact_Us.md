## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an account to use VoxNote?**
No. VoxNote works straight out of the box — no sign-up, no login.

**Does VoxNote work offline?**
Yes. Speech is generated using your device's built-in voices, so it works offline once your language pack is installed.

**Which file types can I import?**
TXT, Markdown (.md), Word (.docx), and PDF.

**Can I keep listening with my screen off?**
Yes. Audio continues in the background with playback controls on your lock screen and notification shade.

**Where are my notes stored?**
On your device only. We never upload your notes or files.
