## Getting Started

1. **Open VoxNote** and tap **Get Started** to complete the short intro.
2. On the **Home** screen, tap the **+ (Add)** button.
3. Choose **Paste text** to paste content from ChatGPT or anywhere, or **Upload File** to import a document.
4. Press **Play** to listen. That's it — no account needed.

---

## How-To Guides

**Convert pasted text to speech**
Tap **+ → Paste text**, paste your content, give it a title, pick a voice, then press **Preview Voice** or save and play.

**Import a document**
Tap **+ → Upload File** and choose a TXT, Markdown (.md), Word (.docx), or PDF file. VoxNote extracts the text automatically.

**Change the voice, speed, or pitch**
Open a note's player, tap the voice name, and choose from your device's available voices. Use the sliders to adjust speed (0.5×–2.0×) and pitch.

**Listen in the background**
Start playback and lock your phone or switch apps — audio keeps playing. Control it from your lock screen or notification shade (play, pause, skip).

**Build a play queue**
Add multiple notes to the queue and they'll play one after another, hands-free.

**Export as MP3**
From the player or note detail screen, tap **Export**, choose your format and quality, then save or share the file.

**Set a sleep timer**
In the player, open the **Sleep Timer** and choose a duration. Playback stops automatically — ideal for bedtime.

## Troubleshooting

**No sound / voice not playing**
- Check your device volume and that silent mode is off.
- Make sure your language voice pack is installed (Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content / Text-to-Speech on your device).

**A voice or language is missing**
Available voices come from your device's operating system. Install additional voices via your device's text-to-speech settings, then reopen VoxNote.

**File won't import**
Confirm the file is a supported type (TXT, MD, DOCX, PDF) and not password-protected or corrupted. Very large files may take a moment to process.

**Export isn't working**
Ensure you have free storage space and that VoxNote has permission to access files.

If your issue isn't covered here, email **support@krivatech.com** with your device model, OS version, and a short description.
